About me

Healthcare leadership meets business analysis

I am a results-driven HealthTech Business Analyst and Team Lead with strong experience in healthcare operations, process improvement, and strategic service delivery. Holding an NVQ Level 5 Diploma in Health and Social Care Management, I combine analytical expertise with hands-on leadership within health and social care environments to deliver efficient, person-centred, and technology-driven solutions.

I have a proven track record of translating complex business and operational needs into practical, data-driven solutions that improve service quality, enhance compliance, reduce costs, and support organisational growth. My experience spans business analysis, stakeholder management, workflow optimisation, process automation, and cross-functional team leadership within healthcare and supported living settings.

As a Team Lead, I excel at coordinating multidisciplinary teams, improving operational performance, implementing innovative digital solutions, and ensuring high standards of care delivery. I am passionate about leveraging technology, analytics, and continuous improvement strategies to enhance both patient outcomes and organisational effectiveness.

I thrive in collaborative environments where strategic thinking, leadership, problem-solving, and innovation are essential to driving sustainable business success.

My approach

How I deliver a project

  1. Discover

    Agree the problem, the stakeholders and what success looks like.

  2. Analyse

    Clean and model the data, then look for the patterns that matter.

  3. Deliver

    Plan the work, lead the team and build dashboards and reports.

  4. Recommend

    Finish with clear next steps in plain English, then measure the result.
